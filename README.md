# Cullery

## About

*Cull + gallery.* A fast, simple photo culler for Windows and Mac, made for RAW + JPEG shoots.

## Why I made this, and what it is

I do a lot of photography. I would spend so much time scrolling through images on my SD card using the native Windows photo app. It was so slow and annoying when I had to scroll through, looking at the JPEGs, and then importing the connected RAW into Lightroom. I have no experience coding apps, so I used Claude to do the coding for me, but every feature and design choice is mine. I needed an app that was fast and combined both the JPEG and RAW into a single viewable image, while still letting me easily drag and drop 50+ RAW images into Lightroom at once. There are some options online for culling images, but many want you to use AI recognition to do the work instead of just looking through photos. Many also have so many features and things to look at. I just wanted to look at the images. That is why I decided to make this app.

I designed this to be simple and easy on the eyes, while still showing everything you need. You simply scroll through the images in a folder and pick or reject what you want. No complicated screens or settings to mess with. You can view either side and decide to drag and drop them into Lightroom or wherever, or delete them all at once. If you have multiple photoshoots saved on the same SD card, you can simply select a range to view only the one you want to work on. This is about as streamlined and simple as I could make it.

## What it does

- **Cull right from the folder.** Open folders directly in Cullery and pick your favorite pictures out of thousands.
- **RAW and JPEG, linked.** Every RAW is automatically linked to its JPEG, so each shot is one photo to look at.
- **Batch export anywhere.** Send your picks directly into Lightroom or any other app, or out to a folder.
- **Built with Rust.** No unnecessary waiting for things to load.
